## Chapter I — The Nation

**Art. 1 — Sovereignty.** The Kingdom of Lanio is a sovereign digital nation. Its motto is **"By The People For The People."** All state institutions exist to serve the citizens, never the reverse.

**Art. 2 — National symbols.** The national animal is the **swan**. The national currency is the **elnina (Ɇ)**, issued and ledgered exclusively by Lanio Trapeza. The national flag follows the Nordic cross tradition of the Scandinavian kingdoms (see the Strategic Blueprint, §2 National Profile).

**Art. 3 — Canonical register.** The Kingdom's founding record is the national register (NationStates: *The Kingdom of Lanio*, home region **Europeia**, founded September 2026). The register is the authoritative chronicle of the Founding Era; Lanio Graphi mirrors it as the internal legal archive.

**Art. 4 — National character.** The Kingdom is classified a **Scandinavian Liberal Paradise**: civil rights at **World Benchmark**, political freedom **Excellent**, economy Reasonable. This classification is normative — policy drift away from benchmark civil rights is unconstitutional.

**Art. 5 — Stewardship.** During the Founding Era the Founder acts as Steward of the Kingdom, holding executive authority in trust. Upon the activation of Lanio Psifos (Future Service Pipeline), elected institutions succeed to that authority.

## Chapter II — Rights and Freedoms

**Art. 6 — Freedom of expression.** Expression critical of religion, the state, and any institution is protected speech. The founding test case arrived when cults demanded the state's money: the Kingdom refused, and open criticism of religion on the airwaves followed — speech, not offense. *(Founder's account, confirmed: "Cults wanted money given to cults" — refused. Register: "radio shows frequently feature people denouncing religion.")*

**Art. 7 — Broadcast standards.** Broadcasting is regulated leniently; risqué programming is lawful and the state does not maintain a censorship regime beyond the escrow and identity rules of the platform. *(Founding Law: "Rupert Bear is considered to be the most risqué TV programme in Lanio." Founder's account: **not recalled** — this article remains provisional until the Founder remembers the underlying issue.)*

**Art. 8 — Secularity.** The state allocates **0.0%** of the founding budget to spirituality and confers no privilege on any confession. The founding decision stands as precedent: when cults demanded public money, the Kingdom refused — no confession, sect, or cult receives state funds. Criticism of religion is speech, not offense.

**Art. 9 — Political participation.** Every verified citizen may petition (Graphi), and upon Psifos activation, vote and stand in elections. Political freedom is rated **Excellent** and must remain so.

**Art. 10 — Property and enterprise.** Private industry is permitted within a market-based economy *(standing policy: Capitalism)*. Citizen assets held on the platform are protected by escrow and the append-only Trapeza ledger.

**Art. 11 — Social welfare.** Welfare is a joint-first budget priority (17.4%). The state guarantees support before it demands order — a liberal paradise is measured by how it treats those with the least.

## Chapter III — The Economy

**Art. 12 — Currency and ledger.** The elnina (Ɇ) is the sole legal tender of the Kingdom. All balances are recorded in Lanio Trapeza's append-only ledger, checkpointed daily on TON. Tampering with the ledger is the gravest economic crime.

**Art. 13 — Market economy.** Private industry is permitted within a market-based economy. The state participates as regulator and escrow agent, not as monopolist.

**Art. 14 — Regulated goods.** Cannabis may be legally purchased *(standing policy: Cannabis)*. All trade — without exception — passes through the state escrow of Lanio Emporio; direct peer-to-peer transfers are barred.

**Art. 15 — Escrow supremacy.** Every commercial transaction follows the escrow state machine defined in the Strategic Blueprint §3. No citizen may be deprived of elninas without a completed escrow flow, a moderator ruling, or a Diki verdict.

## Chapter IV — Education and the Young

**Art. 16 — Education.** Education receives 14.4% of the founding budget — the third-largest allocation. An informed citizenry is the foundation of "By The People For The People."

**Art. 17 — Corporal punishment prohibited; incentive-based pedagogy.** Teachers may not strike children. The classroom disciplines by reward, never violence: even the unruly young receive gold stars rather than the rod. *(Founder's account, approximate: "maybe it was about making so teachers can't beat kids." Register: "five-year-olds who refuse to line up on command get gold stars.")*

## Chapter V — Justice and Order

**Art. 18 — Cyber-capable enforcement.** Law and order receives 9.1% of the founding budget. The founding decision: the Kingdom hires **tech-savvy police to patrol the dark web** — enforcement follows crime into the digital underground, and undercover operations are lawful. *(Founder's account, confirmed: "I hired tech-savvy police to stalk the dark web." Register: "drug deals return to the streets as legions of undercover officers stalk the dark web.")*

**Art. 19 — The Maxtopia Precedent (Exile).** The founding case, confirmed: a pop singer who behaved wrongly was **deported to a nation that sought to put him to death** — the Kingdom returned him to foreign justice rather than shelter him from it. This is codified as the **Maxtopia Doctrine**: the gravest sanction of the Kingdom is exile — dangerous criminals are "accidentally" sent on one-way trips to **Maxtopia**. In the digital state, every ban issued by Lanio moderators is recorded, in-lore, as a one-way trip to Maxtopia. Banishment is the Kingdom's capital punishment — there is no harsher penalty, and no physical harm may ever be threatened. *(Register: "dangerous criminals are often 'accidentally' sent on one-way trips to Maxtopia.")*

**Art. 20 — Defence.** Defence receives 4.5% of the founding budget. The Kingdom renounces weapons of mass destruction *(standing policy: No WMDs)* and wages no wars of conquest; its only army is its moderation staff.

**Art. 21 — Disputes.** Commercial disputes route through the escrow dispute process and its moderators. Upon activation of Lanio Diki, they escalate to the courts, with the precedent archive housed in Graphi.

## Chapter VI — The World

**Art. 22 — Home region.** The Kingdom's home region on the world stage is **Europeia**. It observes World Assembly votes and engages in regional diplomacy *(canon: the Kingdom voted on WA Resolution "Repeal 'Liberate Empires Forever United'", and was endorsed by Le Libertia; regional influence rising from Newcomer to Nipper)*.

**Art. 23 — Foreign digital nations.** Relations with other digital nations (recognition, cross-nation trade, dual citizenship) route through Lanio Presveia upon its activation. Until then, the Founder-Steward speaks for the Kingdom abroad.

## Chapter VII — Amendment

**Art. 24 — Founding Era.** During the Founding Era the Founder-Steward amends this Codex by resolving national issues on the canonical register. Each resolution is appended to Schedule A with its timestamp.

**Art. 25 — Constitutional maturity.** Once the citizen registry exceeds one hundred verified citizens, amendments to Chapters I, II and VII require a Psifos referendum. The Steward's executive authority devolves to elected institutions per Art. 5.
