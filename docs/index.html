The law of the land

The Foundational Codex: seven chapters, twenty-five articles, adopted in a single sitting on 4 October 2026. Its structure follows the constitutional tradition of the Scandinavian kingdoms.

Chapter I. The Nation

Art. 1. Sovereignty. The Kingdom of Lanio is a sovereign nation. Its motto is “By The People For The People.” All institutions of the Kingdom exist to serve the citizens, never the reverse.

Art. 2. National symbols. The national animal is the swan. The national currency is the elnina (Ɇ), issued and ledgered exclusively by the Kingdom’s treasury. The national flag follows the Nordic cross tradition, a warm vermilion field bearing a golden cross lined in cream: the warmth of the people, the light of Lanio, the swan upon the water.

Art. 3. The Founding Register. The founding record of the Kingdom is the Founding Register, kept by the state archive (Graphi). Every act of state (every decree, law, and verdict) is entered into the Register with its date. The Register is the chronicle of the Kingdom; what is not entered did not happen.

Art. 4. National character. The Kingdom holds the civil rights of its citizens to be of the highest standard in the world, and its political freedom to be excellent. This character is normative: drift away from benchmark civil rights is unconstitutional.

Art. 5. Stewardship. During the Founding Era the Founder acts as Steward of the Kingdom, holding executive authority in trust. Upon the activation of the Kingdom’s electoral institutions, elected bodies succeed to that authority.

The economy

The elnina (Ɇ) is the sole legal tender. Balances live in an append-only ledger, checkpointed daily against a public cryptographic anchor, and all trade closes through the state vault.

Escrow, step by step

A citizen commissions a pixel portrait for Ɇ 120 on Emporio. Walk the same state machine the treasury runs. No path ends in limbo.

escrow #0117 · listing live

vault: empty · listing live on Emporio

No funds move until the buyer pays into the vault. Peer-to-peer transfers are barred (Art. 14).

day 1 08:00:00 marketplace citizen listed a pixel portrait commission at Ɇ 120 on Emporio

  • Sellers cannot be held hostage: a buyer who ghosts after delivery gets auto-release after 7 days, not an infinite hold.
  • Buyers cannot be raced: the 72h delivery deadline auto-refunds before the buyer even needs to notice.
  • Disputes always terminate: every path ends in refund, release, or split. There is no funds-locked-forever state.
  • Every transition is an append-only event on the Trapeza log, so the full lifecycle of any escrow stays auditable.

The elnina, in the ledger

Trapeza treats tampering as the gravest economic crime (Art. 12), so the ledger is built to make silent tampering publicly detectable.

Ledger
Append-only event log. Inserts only, no updates, no deletes.
Idempotency
Every transfer carries a server-issued tx_id and a per-account nonce.
Checkpoints
A Merkle hash of the day’s ledger, anchored publicly once per day.
Balances
Derived views of the event log, never stored as editable numbers.
Direct transfers
Barred between citizens. All value moves through escrow (Art. 14).

The roadmap

Six services built at once is how digital nations die in beta. Lanio builds in three phases, and each phase has a definition of done.

  1. Phase 1 · Foundation

    Cosmos + Agora

    under construction

    Identity first, then a voice. Citizens register once and speak across both protocols, Telegram and Matrix, bridged live.

    Success: Citizens can register once and talk across both protocols for 30 days without an identity incident.

  2. Phase 2 · Economy

    Trapeza + Emporio

    Money enters only after identity holds: the append-only ledger, the daily checkpoints, and the escrow state machine above.

    Success: The first 100 escrows close, zero lost funds.

  3. Phase 3 · Statehood

    Graphi + Choros

    Law and land, once there are citizens to govern. The registry deepens, petitions open, and the map fills in.

    Success: Governance and cartography in live use by verified citizens.
