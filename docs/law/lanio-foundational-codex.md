# 📜 The Foundational Codex of the Kingdom of Lanio

> **The Law of the Land · Founding Session of 4 October 2026.**
> This Codex codifies the decisions of the Founding Session, taken in a single sitting on the morning of 4 October 2026, and stands as the supreme law of the Kingdom of Lanio. Its structure follows the constitutional tradition of the Scandinavian kingdoms: chapters, terse articles, amendment rules. The Steward's founding record is preserved in the Graphi internal archive; this public edition states the law of the land.

---

## Chapter I — The Nation

**Art. 1 — Sovereignty.** The Kingdom of Lanio is a sovereign nation. Its motto is **"By The People For The People."** All institutions of the Kingdom exist to serve the citizens, never the reverse.

**Art. 2 — National symbols.** The national animal is the **swan**. The national currency is the **elnina (Ɇ)**, issued and ledgered exclusively by the Kingdom's treasury. The national flag follows the Nordic cross tradition: a warm vermilion field bearing a golden cross lined in cream — the warmth of the people, the light of Lanio, the swan upon the water.

**Art. 3 — The Founding Register.** The founding record of the Kingdom is the **Founding Register**, kept by the state archive (Graphi). Every act of state — every decree, law, and verdict — is entered into the Register with its date. The Register is the chronicle of the Kingdom; what is not entered did not happen.

**Art. 4 — National character.** The Kingdom holds the civil rights of its citizens to be of the highest standard in the world, and its political freedom to be excellent. This character is normative: drift away from benchmark civil rights is unconstitutional.

**Art. 5 — Stewardship.** During the Founding Era the Founder acts as **Steward of the Kingdom**, holding executive authority in trust. Upon the activation of the Kingdom's electoral institutions, elected bodies succeed to that authority.

## Chapter II — Rights and Freedoms

**Art. 6 — Freedom of expression.** Expression critical of religion, the state, and any institution is protected speech. The founding test arrived when cults demanded the Kingdom's money: the Kingdom refused them, and criticism of every faith flourished in the open — speech, not offense.

**Art. 7 — Broadcast standards.** Broadcasting is regulated leniently; risqué programming is lawful, and the Kingdom maintains no censorship office beyond the escrow and identity rules of the platform. *(Founding Session, decision unrecorded in the Register.)*

**Art. 8 — Secularity.** The Kingdom is secular. It confers no privilege on any confession, and no confession, sect, or cult receives public funds. Criticism of religion is speech, not offense.

**Art. 9 — Political participation.** Every verified citizen may petition, and upon the activation of the Kingdom's electoral institutions, vote and stand for office. Political freedom must remain excellent.

**Art. 10 — Property and enterprise.** Private industry is permitted within a market-based economy. Citizen assets held on the Kingdom's platform are protected by escrow and by the append-only public ledger.

**Art. 11 — Social welfare.** Welfare is a joint-first priority of the founding budget. The Kingdom guarantees support before it demands order — a nation is measured by how it treats those with the least.

## Chapter III — The Economy

**Art. 12 — Currency and ledger.** The elnina (Ɇ) is the sole legal tender of the Kingdom. All balances are recorded in the treasury's append-only ledger, checkpointed daily against public cryptographic anchor. Tampering with the ledger is the gravest economic crime.

**Art. 13 — Market economy.** Private industry is permitted within a market-based economy. The Kingdom participates as regulator and escrow agent, never as monopolist.

**Art. 14 — Regulated goods.** Cannabis may be legally purchased. All trade — without exception — passes through the state escrow; direct peer-to-peer transfers between citizens are barred.

**Art. 15 — Escrow supremacy.** Every commercial transaction follows the escrow process of the Kingdom, with delivery deadlines, confirmation windows, and dispute resolution. No citizen may be deprived of elninas without a completed escrow flow, a ruling of the moderators, or a verdict of the courts.

## Chapter IV — Education and the Young

**Art. 16 — Education.** Education is a leading priority of the founding budget. An informed citizenry is the foundation of "By The People For The People."

**Art. 17 — The child may not be struck.** Teachers may not strike children. The classroom disciplines by reward, never by violence: even the unruly young receive gold stars, never the rod.

## Chapter V — Justice and Order

**Art. 18 — Enforcement in the digital underground.** The Kingdom's peace officers are cyber-capable: tech-savvy police patrol the dark web, and undercover operations are lawful. Enforcement follows crime wherever it goes.

**Art. 19 — Exile of the Irredeemable.** When a person has done something so wrong, so evil, that the Kingdom can no longer count them among its people, they shall be **exiled and deported to the country where they will face the worst treatment** — there to answer for what they have done. The founding case: a singer who behaved wickedly was returned to a nation that sought to put him to death, and the Kingdom did not shelter him from it. Exile is the gravest penalty of the Kingdom; the state itself inflicts no physical harm. In the digital realm, exile is enacted as permanent banishment from all services of the Kingdom.

**Art. 20 — Defence.** The Kingdom renounces weapons of mass destruction and wages no wars of conquest. Its only standing force is its moderation staff.

**Art. 21 — Disputes.** Commercial disputes route through the escrow dispute process and its moderators. Upon the activation of the Kingdom's courts, they escalate to the judiciary, and their precedents are entered into the Register.

## Chapter VI — The World

**Art. 22 — Foreign relations.** The Kingdom engages in diplomacy with foreign nations as an equal. Recognition of foreign citizens, cross-nation trade, and dual citizenship are matters of state, conducted through the Kingdom's foreign office when constituted. Until then, the Steward speaks for the Kingdom abroad.

**Art. 23 — Digital nations.** The Kingdom recognizes other digital nations as legitimate subjects of diplomacy, and extends to them the same courtesies it expects: non-interference, honest dealing, and respect for citizens.

## Chapter VII — Amendment

**Art. 24 — Founding Era.** During the Founding Era the Steward amends this Codex by decree. Every amendment is entered into the Founding Register with its date and reasoning.

**Art. 25 — Constitutional maturity.** Once the citizen registry exceeds one hundred verified citizens, amendments to Chapters I, II and VII require a national referendum, and the Steward's executive authority devolves to elected institutions per Art. 5.
